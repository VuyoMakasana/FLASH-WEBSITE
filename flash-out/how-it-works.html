The Process

Ordered this morning.
Delivered by tonight.

Flash coordinates three parties in real time — customer, seller, and driver — so same-day clothing delivery actually works.

For Customers

Shop. Order. Track. Receive.

1

Download the App

Get Flash on iOS or Android. Create your account in under two minutes — no credit card required to browse.

2

Browse Local Sellers

Discover curated clothing from verified local sellers in your city. Filter by style, size, and estimated delivery window.

3

Pay Securely

Check out with card or EFT. Your payment is held safely until the delivery is confirmed — you're protected throughout.

4

Track in Real Time

Watch your driver on the live map from pickup to your door. You'll know exactly when your order arrives.

Download Flash
For Sellers

List. Receive. Hand over. Get paid.

1

Apply & List

Apply to become a Flash seller. Once approved, list your clothing items with photos, descriptions, and same-day availability.

2

Receive Order Alerts

The moment a customer orders, you're notified instantly via the seller dashboard. No manual checking required.

3

Pack & Hand Over

Pack the item and wait for the Flash driver to collect from your location. No drop-offs, no scheduling — the driver comes to you.

4

Get Paid

Payment is released to your seller wallet once delivery is confirmed. Withdraw to your bank account at any time.

Become a Seller
For Drivers

Accept. Collect. Deliver. Earn.

1

Sign Up & Verify

Register as a Flash driver, submit your licence and vehicle details, and pass a quick background check.

2

Go Online

Open the driver app and set yourself as available. Orders in your area are matched to you automatically in real time.

3

Collect & Deliver

Navigate to the seller's address, collect the parcel, and deliver it to the customer. The app guides you every step of the way.

4

Confirm & Earn

Confirm delivery in the app. Your earnings are credited instantly to your Flash wallet — withdraw whenever you like.
