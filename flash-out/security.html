Trust & Safety

Security at Flash

Last updated: 1 June 2025

At Flash, the security of your data, your account, and your payments is a foundational commitment — not an afterthought. This page explains the measures we take to keep you safe, and what you can do to protect yourself.

1. Data Encryption

All data transmitted between your device and Flash servers is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher. This includes all API communications from the Flash mobile app and website. Sensitive data stored on our servers — including personal information and payment records — is encrypted at rest using AES-256.

2. Payment Security

Flash does not store credit card numbers or bank account details on our servers. All payment processing is handled by a PCI DSS Level 1 compliant payment provider. When you enter payment details in the Flash app, those details are transmitted directly to our payment processor via a secure tokenised connection. Flash receives only a masked reference token — never your full card data.

3. Account Security

Your Flash account is protected by:

  • Password hashing using bcrypt with a strong work factor — we never store plaintext passwords
  • Rate limiting on login attempts to prevent brute-force attacks
  • Secure session management with token expiry
  • Account activity notifications for login from new devices

We strongly recommend using a unique, strong password for your Flash account and enabling device-level authentication (Face ID, fingerprint, or PIN) on your phone.

4. Infrastructure Security

Flash infrastructure is hosted on reputable cloud providers with certifications including ISO 27001 and SOC 2. Our servers operate within private networks with strict firewall rules. Access to production systems is limited to authorised engineers, controlled via multi-factor authentication and role-based access controls. We maintain audit logs of all administrative actions.

5. Driver Verification

All Flash drivers undergo identity verification and a background check before being approved to carry deliveries. This includes verification of their South African ID or passport, driver's licence, and vehicle registration. Drivers who fail our standards are not onboarded, and approved drivers can be suspended or removed if standards are breached.

6. Fraud Prevention

We monitor transactions in real time for unusual patterns that may indicate fraud. Our payment processor applies 3D Secure authentication where required. Suspicious activity triggers automatic holds and manual review. If you notice any unauthorised charges or account activity, contact us immediately at security@flashdelivery.co.za.

7. Vulnerability Disclosure

If you discover a security vulnerability in the Flash platform, we ask that you report it to us responsibly rather than publicly disclosing it before we have had an opportunity to address it. Please email security@flashdelivery.co.za with a description of the issue, steps to reproduce it, and any relevant screenshots or logs. We will acknowledge receipt within 48 hours and aim to resolve confirmed vulnerabilities promptly. We appreciate the security community's help in keeping Flash safe.

8. What You Can Do

  • Use a unique, strong password for your Flash account
  • Never share your account credentials with anyone
  • Enable lock-screen authentication on your device
  • Log out of shared devices after use
  • Contact us immediately at security@flashdelivery.co.za if you suspect unauthorised access

9. Contact

For security concerns, vulnerability reports, or account safety issues, email security@flashdelivery.co.za. For general support, visit our Contact page.
