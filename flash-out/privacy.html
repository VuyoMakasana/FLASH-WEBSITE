Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2025  ·  Effective: 1 June 2025

Flash (Pty) Ltd ("Flash", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect information in relation to our mobile application, website (www.flashdelivery.co.za), and related services (collectively, the "Platform"). It also describes your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 4 of 2013 ("POPIA") and other applicable South African law.

By using our Platform, you consent to the collection and use of your personal information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our services.

1. Who We Are

Flash (Pty) Ltd is a company registered in South Africa. We operate as a responsible party (as defined by POPIA) in relation to personal information collected through the Platform. Our registered contact for privacy matters is: privacy@flashdelivery.co.za.

2. What Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

  • Account registration details (name, email address, phone number, password)
  • Delivery address and location information
  • Payment information (processed securely by our payment provider — we do not store card numbers)
  • Profile information and preferences
  • Communications you send to us, including support requests

2.2 Information we collect automatically

  • Device information (device type, operating system, unique device identifiers)
  • App usage data and interaction logs
  • Location data (with your permission) — used to match you with nearby sellers and drivers
  • IP address and network information
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see our Cookie Policy)

2.3 Information from third parties

  • Payment processors (transaction confirmation and fraud signals)
  • Identity and background verification providers (for driver onboarding)
  • Analytics providers

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Create and manage your account on the Platform
  • Process and fulfil orders, including matching customers with sellers and dispatching drivers
  • Process payments and prevent fraud
  • Provide customer support and resolve disputes
  • Send transactional communications (order confirmations, delivery updates)
  • Send marketing communications where you have given consent (you may opt out at any time)
  • Improve and personalise the Platform based on usage patterns
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Enforce our Terms and Conditions and protect the rights of users

4. Location Data

The Flash app requires access to your device's location in order to show you nearby sellers and to enable drivers to navigate to your delivery address. Location access is permission-based — you grant this via your device settings. You may withdraw location access at any time, though this will limit the functionality of the Platform. Driver location is tracked in real time during active deliveries to enable the live tracking feature for customers.

5. Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses cookies to improve your experience and to understand how the site is used. Please see our full Cookie Policy for details. We use analytics tools including Google Analytics to understand traffic patterns. These tools may set their own cookies. You can opt out of analytics tracking via your browser settings or our cookie preferences.

6. Who We Share Your Information With

We share your personal information only as necessary:

  • Sellers: We share your delivery address and contact number with the seller fulfilling your order.
  • Drivers: We share your delivery address and first name with the driver assigned to your order.
  • Payment processors: Payment data is processed by our PCI-DSS compliant payment partner. We do not store full card details.
  • Service providers: We use third-party vendors for cloud hosting, analytics, and customer support tools. These parties process data only under our instruction.
  • Legal compliance: We may disclose information where required by law, court order, or regulatory authority.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not share your information with advertisers.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period thereafter to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Inactive accounts are reviewed periodically. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (subject to legal retention requirements) by emailing privacy@flashdelivery.co.za.

8. Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These include encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and regular security reviews. For more detail, see our Security page. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights Under POPIA

As a data subject under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Know whether we hold your personal information
  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal obligations)
  • Object to the processing of your personal information
  • Submit a complaint to the Information Regulator of South Africa

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@flashdelivery.co.za. We will respond within 30 days.

The Information Regulator of South Africa can be contacted at: inforegulator.org.za.

10. Children's Privacy

The Flash Platform is not intended for use by persons under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we become aware that we have collected information from a minor without parental consent, we will delete it promptly. Please contact us at privacy@flashdelivery.co.za if you believe this has occurred.

11. Cross-Border Transfers

Where we transfer personal information outside South Africa (for example, to cloud service providers), we do so only where there is an adequate level of data protection in place, consistent with POPIA's requirements. We will inform you of such transfers where relevant.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified via the app or by email. Continued use of the Platform after changes are notified constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy. We record the effective date of each version at the top of this page.

13. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your personal information, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@flashdelivery.co.za
  • General: hello@flashdelivery.co.za
  • Website: flashdelivery.co.za/contact
