Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 1 June 2025

Flash (Pty) Ltd is committed to ensuring that our website and mobile application are accessible to everyone — including people with disabilities. We believe that great technology should work for all users, and we actively work to meet and maintain Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards.

1. Our Accessibility Features

The Flash website is designed and built with the following accessibility features:

  • Keyboard navigation: All interactive elements — links, buttons, forms, and the mobile menu — are fully operable using a keyboard alone. Tab order follows a logical sequence. Focus is always visible.
  • Screen reader support: We use semantic HTML5 elements (header, nav, main, footer, section, article) throughout. ARIA labels and roles are applied where native semantics are insufficient. Dynamic content changes are announced via ARIA live regions.
  • Skip navigation: A "Skip to main content" link is available at the top of every page, allowing keyboard and screen reader users to bypass repeated navigation.
  • Colour contrast: Text and interactive elements are designed to meet or exceed WCAG AA contrast ratios (4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text and UI components).
  • Responsive design: Content reflows correctly at all viewport sizes and zoom levels up to 400%, without requiring horizontal scrolling.
  • Alternative text: All meaningful images include descriptive alt text. Decorative images are marked with empty alt attributes or aria-hidden.
  • Reduced motion: Animations and transitions are disabled automatically when the user has enabled "Reduce Motion" in their operating system preferences.
  • Form accessibility: All form inputs are associated with visible labels. Error messages are programmatically associated with the relevant field and announced to screen readers.
  • Link clarity: All links have descriptive, meaningful text. We avoid "click here" and "read more" links without context.

2. Mobile App Accessibility

The Flash mobile application (iOS and Android) is built to support native platform accessibility features, including:

  • VoiceOver (iOS) and TalkBack (Android) screen reader compatibility
  • Dynamic Type / font scaling support
  • High contrast mode compatibility
  • Touch target sizing meeting minimum 44×44pt guidelines

3. Known Limitations

We are a growing platform and, despite our best efforts, some areas may not yet fully meet all WCAG 2.1 AA criteria. Known areas we are actively improving include:

  • Some third-party embedded content may not fully meet our accessibility standards — we are working with providers to address this.
  • The live map driver tracking feature uses a third-party mapping component that has limited screen reader support. We provide text-based status updates as an alternative.

We are committed to ongoing improvement and conduct regular accessibility reviews.

4. Feedback and Contact

If you experience any difficulty accessing any part of the Flash website or mobile app, or if you notice an accessibility issue we have missed, please contact us. Your feedback directly informs our accessibility work.

  • Email: accessibility@flashdelivery.co.za
  • General contact: flashdelivery.co.za/contact

We aim to respond to all accessibility feedback within 5 business days.

5. Enforcement

Flash is committed to resolving all accessibility barriers reported to us within a reasonable timeframe. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the South African Human Rights Commission at sahrc.org.za.
