Boilerplate

Flash is South Africa's same-day clothing delivery platform, connecting customers with local clothing sellers through a network of independent drivers. Founded in Cape Town in 2024 by Vuyo Makasana, Flash enables same-day delivery of fashion and clothing items within South African cities. Flash currently operates in Cape Town and Johannesburg, with further city expansion planned.
