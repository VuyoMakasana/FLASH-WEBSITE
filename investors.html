The infrastructure
Africa's commerce needs.

The Opportunity

A market waiting to be unlocked.

Market Size

R100B+ SA clothing market

South Africa's fashion retail sector is one of the largest in Africa — and virtually untouched by same-day delivery infrastructure. Flash is positioned to become the category default.

Expansion

Pan-African scale

The Flash model — sellers, drivers, customers in one coordinated platform — is replicable across Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana, and beyond. South Africa is the proof point.

Network Effect

Three-sided flywheel

More sellers attract more customers. More customers attract more drivers. More drivers enable more sellers. The network compounds — and creates a durable moat.

Why Now

The conditions have never been more aligned.

Smartphone penetration is at an all-time high in South Africa. E-commerce adoption accelerated through the pandemic. Independent sellers are looking for better platforms. Customers expect faster delivery than ever.

Flash exists at the intersection of all four trends — and we're building the infrastructure before a global player can.

Early-Stage Advantage

Flash is at the stage where investor capital has the highest leverage — before scale, before competition locks the market, and before valuations reflect the full opportunity.

Founder-Led, Capital-Efficient

Flash is built lean, with a deep understanding of the South African market. Investment goes into growth — not excess. Our capital efficiency is a feature, not a constraint.

Defensible Technology

Our dispatch algorithm, seller dashboard, and driver app form a proprietary tech stack built specifically for the South African logistics context — not a copy-paste from the West.

I started Flash because I watched brilliant local sellers in Port Elizabeth lose sales every day — not because of their product, but because they had no fast, reliable way to get clothing to their customers. That's the problem we solve. And the scale of that problem, across South Africa and Africa, is enormous. We're looking for investors who want to be part of building the infrastructure that makes it possible.
— Vuyo Makasana, Founder & CEO
