Support

Frequently asked questions.

Can't find what you're looking for? Contact us directly — we respond within one business day.

For Customers
Flash is a same-day clothing delivery platform that connects customers with local clothing sellers in South Africa. You browse clothing in the Flash app, place an order, and a nearby Flash driver picks it up from the seller and delivers it to your door — all on the same day.
Flash is currently launching in Cape Town and Johannesburg. We're expanding to additional South African cities throughout the year — sign up in the app to be notified when Flash arrives in your city.
Orders placed before our daily cut-off time (shown in the app) are delivered the same day, typically within 2–4 hours of placing your order. Exact windows depend on seller location and driver availability in your area.
Once your order is collected by a driver, you'll see them on a live map in the Flash app. You'll also receive push notifications at key milestones — order confirmed, driver assigned, driver en route, and delivery confirmed.
Flash accepts major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard) as well as instant EFT. Your payment is held securely until delivery is confirmed — you're protected throughout the transaction.
Contact our support team at support@flashdelivery.co.za immediately. We investigate every issue and will arrange a resolution — whether that's a replacement delivery, a refund, or working with the seller to correct the problem.
Yes — downloading and browsing Flash is completely free. A delivery fee is charged per order, which is shown clearly at checkout before you confirm your purchase. There are no subscription fees or hidden charges.
For Sellers
Any clothing seller based in a Flash city can apply — home-based sellers, boutiques, or online brands. You need to be able to prepare orders for driver pickup within a reasonable time window. Apply here.
Flash charges a single transparent commission per sale. The exact rate is shared during the seller onboarding process. There are no listing fees, monthly charges, or surprise deductions — you always know what you'll receive before you list.
No. Flash handles all logistics. Once an order comes in, you pack the item and a Flash driver comes to collect it from your location. You don't need to leave your premises or manage any delivery coordination.
Seller earnings are reconciled weekly and paid directly to your registered South African bank account. You can also view your earnings in real time via the seller dashboard.
For Drivers
Flash accepts cars, motorbikes, and bakkies. Your vehicle must be roadworthy, registered, and insured. You'll also need a valid South African driver's licence (Code 08 or above) and a smartphone to run the driver app.
Earnings depend on the number of deliveries you complete and your city. Flash pays a per-delivery rate, and top-rated drivers receive access to priority orders and bonus incentives. Exact earnings figures are shared during onboarding.
Every completed delivery credits your Flash wallet instantly. You can withdraw to your South African bank account at any time — there are no minimum withdrawal thresholds or holding periods.
Yes. Flash drivers are independent and set their own availability. Go online when you want to take orders, and offline whenever you need to stop. There are no minimum hour requirements.
