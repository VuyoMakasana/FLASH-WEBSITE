Why Drive with Flash

Built around your life.

Completely Flexible

Go online and offline whenever you choose. Drive for two hours or ten — the platform fits around your life, not the other way around.

Instant Earnings

Every completed delivery credits your Flash wallet immediately. Withdraw to your South African bank account any time — no holding periods.

Short, Local Routes

Flash deliveries are hyperlocal — same-city routes that keep you in your neighbourhood, minimising fuel costs and maximising delivery frequency.

Smart Matching

Our dispatch algorithm routes orders to the nearest available driver — so you spend less time waiting and more time earning.

Reputation Rewards

Build your rating with every delivery. Top-rated drivers get priority access to high-value orders and bonus incentives.

Dedicated Support

In-app driver support is available whenever you're on the road. Issues with an order? Our team responds fast.

Requirements

What you need to get started.

Flash driver requirements are straightforward. If you have a vehicle and a valid licence, you're most of the way there.

  • Valid South African driver's licence

    Code 08 or above.

  • Roadworthy vehicle

    Car, motorbike, or bakkie. Must have valid registration and insurance.

  • Android or iOS smartphone

    To run the Flash Driver app.

  • South African bank account

    For earnings withdrawals.

  • Clear background check

    We run a standard verification during onboarding.
